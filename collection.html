Greco-roman godess

Ceres

Greek: Demeter

Domain: Agriculture & Harvest

Role: Celebrated by farmers to ensure bountiful crops and fertility of the earth.

Jupiter

Greek: Zeus

Domain: Sky, Lightning & Justice

Role: Supreme ruler and guardian of the state, laws, and military victory.

Mars

Greek: Ares

Domain: War & Agriculture

Role: Protector of Rome’s legions and legendary ancestor of the Roman people.

Minerva

Greek: Athena

Domain: Wisdom, Strategy & Craft

Role: Patron of artisans, scholars, and thoughtful military planning.

Neptune

Greek: Poseidon

Domain: Oceans, Seas & Earthquakes

Role: Invoked by sailors and merchants for safe passage across the waters.

Proserpina

Greek: Persephone

Domain: Underworld & Spring

Role: Symbol of cyclical renewal and the return of spring blooms.

Juno

Greek: Hera

Domain: Marriage & Childbirth

Role: Protector of women and counselor to the Roman state.

Venus

Greek: Aphrodite

Domain: Love, Beauty & Prosperity

Role: Revering divine harmony, passion, and the ancestral heritage of Rome.

Apollo

Greek: Apollon

Domain: Sun, Music & Prophecy

Role: Patron of poetry, truth, medicine, and divine foresight.

Bacchus

Greek: Dionysus

Domain: Wine, Revelry & Nature

Role: Honored during festive mysteries to inspire joy, theater, and liberation.

Cybele

Greek: Rhea

Domain: Earth, Mountains & Wildlife

Role: Celebrated as Magna Mater (Great Mother) defending the Roman realm.

Diana

Greek: Artemis

Domain: The Hunt, Forest & Moon

Role: Guardian of wild nature, maidenhood, and wanderers in the woods.

Mercury

Greek: Hermes

Domain: Commerce, Travel & Messages

Role: Fast messenger guiding merchants in trade and travelers on their journey.

Vesta

Greek: Hestia

Domain: Hearth, Home & Sacred Fire

Role: Preserved by the Vestal Virgins to keep Rome’s eternal flame burning.

Vulcan

Greek: Hephaestus

Domain: Fire, Forges & Metalwork

Role: Crafted legendary armor for the gods and protected against destructive fire.
